Document Name: Product Overview

Purpose:
Explain the purpose of the TEJ PIM platform.

Scope:
Product purpose, business problem, scope boundaries, documented users, implemented features, and project terminology validated from TEJ PIM sources.

Content:
Product Name: TEJ PIM

Validated Product Description:
India-first, multi-tenant Product Information Management platform with connector-first marketplace architecture.
Source: docs/product/PRD.md

Business Problem Definition:
- Fragmented product data management across spreadsheets and channel portals.
- High rejection rates due to missing required fields and channel-specific constraints.
- Slow onboarding and error-prone bulk updates.
- Limited readiness and operational risk visibility.
Source: docs/product/PRD.md

Current Product Scope:
In scope (validated in PRD and API modules):
- Product, category, and variant catalog operations.
- Compliance validation and issue persistence.
- Channel readiness simulation and structured issue normalization.
- Bulk import templates/jobs/retries/row repair.
- Bulk edit with preview and operation history.
- Connector install/credential/export/publish orchestration paths.

Out of scope (PRD current cycle):
- OMS/order lifecycle replacement.
- Full DAM intelligence automation beyond current increment scope.
- AI-led autonomous optimization as launch dependency.

Documented Users and Roles:
- Admin
- Editor
- Viewer
- Marketplace Ops
Source: docs/product/PRD.md

Implemented Feature Baseline (validated from code and openapi):
