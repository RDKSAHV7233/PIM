# Vexa PIM Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

- Document owner: Legal + CTO
- Status: Operational baseline pending customer-specific annexes
- Last updated: 2026-03-19

## 1. Parties and Scope
This Data Processing Agreement applies where the customer uses Vexa PIM and the Vexa PIM operator processes personal data on the customer’s behalf.

This DPA supplements the applicable:
- order form;
- pilot agreement;
- master services agreement; or
- Terms of Service.

If there is a conflict, the customer agreement governs first, then this DPA, then general product documentation.

## 2. Roles
- Customer: controller or business owner
- Vexa PIM operator: processor or service provider, except where the operator acts under an independent legal obligation

## 3. Subject Matter and Duration
The processing covers personal data necessary to provide Vexa PIM and continues for the duration of the customer relationship, plus any lawful retention period required for security, audit, or compliance.

## 4. Nature and Purpose of Processing
Processing may include:
- storing and organizing catalog and workspace data;
- authenticating users;
- enforcing role-based access and tenant isolation;
- maintaining audit logs and security controls;
- recording privacy requests and consent events;
- executing connector workflows at the customer’s direction; and
- supporting troubleshooting, incident response, and operational maintenance.

## 5. Categories of Data Subjects
Data subjects may include:
- customer employees and contractors;
- workspace administrators;
- approved marketplace operators;
- support contacts; and
- other users authorized by the customer.

## 6. Categories of Personal Data
Personal data may include:
- account identifiers and role assignments;
- authentication and security metadata;
- audit trail information;
- connector account aliases and administrative metadata;
- privacy request records; and
- any personal data the customer chooses to place inside product or operational fields.

## 7. Customer Instructions
The operator will process personal data only:
- on documented customer instructions;
- as required to provide the service; or
- where required by law.

The customer is responsible for ensuring its instructions are lawful and that it has the right to submit the relevant data to Vexa PIM.

## 8. Confidentiality
The operator must ensure that personnel with access to personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations or equivalent duties.

## 9. Security Measures
The operator will implement technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service stage and risk profile, including measures such as:
- access control;
- tenant-scoped authorization patterns;
- audit logging;
- credential protection and rotation support;
- release verification before broad rollout changes; and
- operational monitoring and incident handling procedures proportionate to current maturity.

## 10. Subprocessors
The operator may engage subprocessors required to run Vexa PIM, subject to appropriate contractual safeguards.

At minimum, subprocessors must be bound to:
- confidentiality;
- security obligations; and
- processing terms no less protective than those in this DPA.

The operator should maintain a current subprocessor list in customer-facing contracting or onboarding materials where required.

## 11. Assistance to Customer
The operator will provide reasonable assistance, taking into account the nature of processing and available functionality, for:
- security incident response;
- privacy request handling;
- export and deletion workflows; and
- compliance documentation reasonably required for the service relationship.

At the current rollout stage, some privacy workflows may involve recorded manual handling rather than automated fulfillment.

## 12. Security Incidents
The operator will notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal-data security incident affecting the customer’s data, subject to verification and operational constraints.

The notice should include available information sufficient to support the customer’s legal and operational response.

## 13. Return and Deletion
Upon termination or documented customer instruction, the operator will return or delete personal data as required by the governing agreement, unless retention is required by law or justified under the Data Retention Policy.

## 14. Audit and Information Rights
The operator will provide reasonable information demonstrating compliance with this DPA, subject to confidentiality, security, and proportionality controls.

Formal audits must be:
- reasonable in scope;
- non-disruptive;
- subject to prior notice; and
- coordinated through the operator.

## 15. International Transfers
If personal data is transferred across borders, the operator will use safeguards appropriate to the applicable legal framework and the customer relationship.

## 16. Liability
Liability under this DPA is subject to the liability terms in the governing customer agreement, unless mandatory law requires otherwise.

## 17. Order of Precedence
This DPA should be interpreted consistently with the customer agreement and the Privacy Policy. Customer-specific commercial or regulatory commitments may be added through annexes or negotiated schedules.
