# Vexa PIM Privacy Policy

- Document owner: Product + Legal + CTO
- Status: Active baseline for controlled rollout
- Last updated: 2026-09-03

## 1. Purpose
This Privacy Policy explains how Vexa PIM handles personal data in connection with the Vexa PIM platform and related controlled-rollout operations.

Vexa PIM is an India-first multi-tenant product information management platform. Most personal data processed in Vexa PIM is customer account, workspace administration, audit, or support data rather than consumer-profile data.

## 2. Roles
In most service scenarios:
- the customer organization acts as the controller or business owner for data it submits to Vexa PIM; and
- the Vexa PIM operator acts as processor or service provider on the customer’s instructions.

The operator may act as an independent controller where required for:
- security logging;
- fraud prevention;
- legal compliance;
- billing and contract administration; or
- incident response.

## 3. Categories of Data We Process
Vexa PIM may process the following categories of data:

### 3.1 Account and workspace data
- user ID or login identifier;
- workspace identifier;
- role and access-control settings;
- password hash where per-user credentials are enabled;
- account lock state and authentication event metadata.

### 3.2 Product and business data
- product titles, descriptions, attributes, variants, category mappings, and media references;
- channel and connector configuration relevant to catalog distribution;
- marketplace submission and export job metadata.

### 3.3 Audit and operational data
- audit events;
- timestamps and actor identifiers;
- queue and job status metadata;
- security and operational error logs; and
- rollout and staging evidence artifacts.

### 3.4 Privacy workflow data
- consent records;
- account export request metadata;
- account deletion request metadata; and
- related admin handling notes where applicable.

## 4. Purposes of Processing
We process data to:
- provide and secure the service;
- authenticate users and enforce tenant isolation;
- enable catalog, connector, and approval workflows;
- fulfill customer instructions;
- maintain auditability and incident response capability;
- comply with legal obligations; and
- improve reliability, scaling, and support operations.

## 5. Legal Basis
Depending on the relationship and applicable law, processing may be based on:
- performance of a contract;
- legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention, and service operations;
- consent where specifically captured;
- legal or regulatory obligations; and
- customer instructions under a service agreement or DPA.

## 6. Security Measures
Vexa PIM uses technical and organizational controls appropriate to the current service stage, including:
- role-based access control;
- tenant-scoped data access patterns;
- audit logging;
- controlled credential handling;
- queue isolation and background job controls; and
- controlled staging verification before broader release progression.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Customers must also manage internal access, least privilege, and credential hygiene.

## 7. Data Sharing
We do not sell customer personal data.

We may share or disclose data:
- with infrastructure and service providers needed to operate Vexa PIM;
- with connectors or marketplaces when the customer initiates export or publish actions;
- with professional advisers bound by confidentiality; or
- where required by law, court order, or regulatory process.

Any subprocessors or third-party processors must be used under appropriate contractual and security controls.

## 8. Data Retention
Retention periods are governed by the Data Retention Policy and may vary by data category, legal hold, customer request, or security need.

Current platform behavior includes:
- audit and privacy-request records retained for operational and compliance traceability;
- test data governance controls and purge workflows for non-production artifacts; and
- manual handling for some privacy-export fulfillment paths during controlled rollout.

## 9. Cross-Border Processing
Depending on infrastructure placement and connector requirements, data may be processed in jurisdictions outside the customer’s principal place of business.

Where cross-border processing occurs, the operator will use contractual, organizational, or legal safeguards appropriate to the applicable law and service arrangement.

## 10. Data Subject and Customer Rights
Depending on applicable law and service role, customers or data subjects may request:
- access;
- correction;
- deletion;
- export;
- restriction;
- objection; or
- withdrawal of consent where consent is the legal basis.

At the current rollout stage:
- consent events are recorded in-product;
- deletion requests can be scheduled in-product;
- account export requests are recorded in-product and handled through an admin-led process until automated fulfillment is implemented.

## 11. Children’s Data
Vexa PIM is intended for business use and is not directed to children. Customers must not use Vexa PIM to intentionally collect or process children’s personal data without a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.

## 12. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. Material changes should be communicated through the service or associated customer communication channels.
