# Seller Interview Script (45 Minutes)

## Interview Goals
- Quantify rejection pain and workflow bottlenecks.
- Validate current tools and switching costs.
- Validate willingness to pay for outcomes.

## Intro (3 minutes)
- Quick context on purpose (research, not sales).
- Confirm permission to take notes.
- Confirm seller profile (size, category, marketplaces).

## Section 1: Current Listing Workflow (8 minutes)
Questions:
1. Walk me through how a product goes from source data to live listing.
2. Which parts are manual vs automated?
3. Who owns catalog updates internally?

## Section 2: Rejection Pain (8 minutes)
Questions:
1. How often do listings get rejected?
2. Top reasons for rejection in the past 3 months?
3. Impact of rejection (time delay, lost sales, operational cost)?

## Section 3: Compliance and Taxonomy (7 minutes)
Questions:
1. How do you currently handle GST/HSN and compliance fields?
2. Which categories are hardest to complete correctly?
3. How do you detect issues before submission?

## Section 4: Bulk Operations and Excel Workflow (7 minutes)
Questions:
1. How many SKUs do you process per upload batch?
2. What breaks most often in Excel/CSV imports?
3. How do you fix failed rows today?

## Section 5: Channel Differences (5 minutes)
Questions:
1. Which channels are most painful and why?
2. How different are field requirements across channels?
3. What would reduce rejection fastest for you?

## Section 6: Tooling and Switching Friction (4 minutes)
Questions:
1. What tools do you use today?
2. What do you like/dislike most?
3. What blocks switching to a new tool?

## Section 7: Pricing and Buying Signals (3 minutes)
Questions:
1. If this reduces rejection significantly, what monthly budget is reasonable?
2. Which pricing tier feels realistic (Starter/Growth/Pro)?
3. Would you join a pilot? Paid or unpaid?

## Closing (3 minutes)
- Ask for referral to 1-2 similar sellers.
- Confirm if they want pilot updates.

## Scoring Rubric
- Pain severity: Low / Medium / High
- Rejection impact: Low / Medium / High
- Buy signal: Low / Medium / High
- Pilot fit: No / Maybe / Yes
