# Pricing Sensitivity Call Script (15 Minutes)

## Purpose
Validate willingness to pay for measurable outcomes: rejection reduction and faster catalog throughput.

## Context Setup
- We are testing pricing for an India-first catalog platform.
- We are not asking for commitment in this call unless the seller is ready.

## Questions
1. What is the monthly cost of listing errors and rework for your team?
2. If rejection rate drops materially, what monthly spend is justified?
3. Which pricing band fits best?
   - Starter: INR 2,999
   - Growth: INR 7,999
   - Pro: INR 14,999
4. Which outcomes are non-negotiable for that spend?
5. What concerns block purchase?
6. Would you join a pilot? If yes, paid or unpaid?

## Capture
- Preferred tier
- Decision confidence (Low/Medium/High)
- Objections
- Conditions to buy
- Pilot intent
