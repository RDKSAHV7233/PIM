# Internal Commercial Operations Specification

This document defines the internal commercial workflow for Vexa's no-public-
pricing rollout. It intentionally omits price amounts. Approved commercial
values must be stored in a restricted price book controlled by Finance and
Sales/RevOps.

## Required CRM Fields

- Plan interest.
- Trial source.
- SKU band.
- Connector requirements.
- Workspace requirements.
- Onboarding requirements.
- Migration complexity.
- Security and SSO needs.
- Discount request.
- Price-book version.
- Proposal or order-form reference.
- Implementation/SOW reference.

## Required Templates

- Proposal template.
- Order form.
- Paid pilot agreement.
- Implementation questionnaire.
- SOW template.
- Discount exception workflow.
- Allowed-claims checklist.

## Commercial Stages

- Trial.
- Paid pilot.
- Starter qualification.
- Growth demo.
- Business discovery.
- Enterprise consultative discovery.
- Subscription.
- Renewal.

## Quote Controls

- Sales must quote only from the active approved price book.
- Every proposal must reference the active price-book version.
- Discount requests outside authority require approval.
- Custom implementation, migration, or connector work requires a separate SOW.
- Enterprise commitments require CTO and Legal review.

## Verification Status

CRM, proposal-system, email-provider, analytics, and external quote tooling
cannot be marked complete from repository evidence alone. Codex must verify
through an authorised interface or report NOT VERIFIED.
